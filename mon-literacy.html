Cultural preservation · Sangkhlaburi, Thailand

Language survives when people build the tools.

Volunteered with the Mon Literacy and Culture Survival Group in Sangkhlaburi, Thailand, helping preserve ancient texts written on bamboo by developing Mon-language fonts that made the material possible to transcribe and share.

A small piece of practical digital infrastructure made endangered knowledge more accessible: language access, community partnership, and cultural survival in one project.
