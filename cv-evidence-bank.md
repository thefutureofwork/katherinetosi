## Mon Culture and Literature Survival Project · Sangkhlaburi, Thailand

**Evidence theme:** Cultural preservation, digital literacy infrastructure, refugee-community engagement

Volunteered with the Mon Culture and Literature Survival Project in Sangkhlaburi, on the Thailand–Myanmar border (2000–2003). Brought the refugee camp’s first internet line in from Thailand, secured the computers monks used to transcribe palm-leaf manuscripts, and built a needs-based, 36-unit bilingual English/Mon curriculum for a stateless refugee community. Worked alongside a respected Mon cultural preservationist living in the camp, who developed the Mon-script fonts and adapted a keyboard so texts and materials could be produced in Mon script.

**CV-ready version:**
> With the Mon Culture and Literature Survival Project in Sangkhlaburi, Thailand, built the project’s digital infrastructure (the camp’s first internet line and the computers monks used to transcribe palm-leaf manuscripts) and a needs-based, 36-unit bilingual English/Mon curriculum, working alongside the Mon cultural preservationist who developed the Mon-script fonts and keyboard.

**Leadership signal:** Practical technology in service of cultural survival; community partnership; language access; preservation of endangered knowledge.
