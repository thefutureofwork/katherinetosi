The practice

I have spent three decades moving between the two ends of education that rarely talk to each other: the pastoral machinery that keeps a struggling student in the building, and the curriculum design that makes the building worth walking into.

At Dulwich College Beijing, I ran professional development and EAL, managed a team of sixteen, lifted senior school attainment 12% in one year, and built the Rolling Professional Development plan for 113 teachers.

At Harrow Shanghai, I built a whole-school student services department from inception, nursery through Year 13, within twelve months. At NYU Shanghai, I chair faculty DEI and Community Connections work, facilitate for the Center for Student Belonging, co-facilitate LEAD mentorship, and serve as Campus Point of Contact for the UN Millennium Fellowship.

In 2026, I co-authored the EAP Academic Program Review Self-Study and wrote the department's strategic vision and five-year plan with the Assistant Dean for Academic Affairs. The vision positions EAP to become the Center for Academic Inquiry, integrating AI literacy, digital literacy, ethical reasoning, and experiential learning.
