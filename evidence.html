What students actually produce

Student Work

The test of a pedagogy is what it produces. These are real artifacts from real students in my EAP courses at NYU Shanghai: reflective journals, research reports, campaign materials, tracking systems, and peer evaluations.

01 

Reflective practice

Reflection is the engine of the Students as Partners approach. Students use the DEIP framework (Describe, Evaluate, Interpret, Plan) to analyze their own learning, group dynamics, and community engagement.

Reflective journal

Volunteering with autistic children at a charity shop

A student reflects on their experiential learning placement, using the DEIP cycle to connect volunteering with classroom concepts about inclusion and social responsibility. Includes photo evidence.

Weekly personal learning and academic journal

The structured DEIP writing framework students use each week to reflect on their learning, group work, and self-direction. Shows how reflection is scaffolded, not left to chance.
