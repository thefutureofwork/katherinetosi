The record

1992 — 1996

German Teacher, Grades 7–12

Springfield High School · Vermont, USA
First teaching position. Secondary German across all year groups in a small rural New England school. Student-taught in the same district with two very different populations: a program for the county's most challenging students (behavior graded 80% on not being violent) and Vietnamese refugee children who arrived speaking zero English. That dual placement is where the interest in SEN, EAL, and student welfare began.
1997 — 1999

Cambridge English & IELTS Instructor

The School for New Languages · Aarau, Switzerland
First international move. Taught Cambridge English exam preparation and IELTS to adult learners in a Swiss language school. Built fluency in working across languages and cultures that would define the next three decades.
2000 — 2003

Columnist, Bangkok Post · Volunteer Literacy Teacher

Bangkok, Thailand · Sangkhlaburi refugee camp
Wrote a weekly column, Kat’s Window on Thailand, interpreting Thai culture for the readership of Asia’s second-largest English newspaper. Simultaneously volunteered with the Mon Culture and Literature Survival Group, developing literacy materials for refugees in Sangkhlaburi. Also worked as a digital content author for katswindow.net.
2003

IELTS Instructor (part-time)

Taylor College · Sydney, Australia
IELTS instruction alongside MA TESOL studies at the University of New South Wales.
2004 — 2006

K–12 English Teacher

International Language School · Mandalay, Myanmar
Taught English across all age groups in Mandalay. First experience designing curriculum for an entire school in a developing-country context.
2006 — 2011

Head of EAL · Head of Pastoral, Years 7–13 · Head of PSHE

Bromsgrove International School · Bangkok, Thailand
International boarding school, CIS accredited
Combined academic and pastoral leadership across the entire secondary school. This is where leadership began in earnest: running both the EAL provision and the pastoral system for Years 7 through 13, plus PSHE.
  • Led EAL curriculum design and in-class support across secondary
  • Chaired the PSHCE CIS committee; member of CIS English committee
  • Organized inter-house events, Secondary Book Week, boarding duties
  • Taught IGCSE EAL, IGCSE Literature, mainstream English, IELTS
2011 — 2016

Professional Development Coordinator · Head of EAL · English Teacher

Dulwich College Beijing · China
British International School of the Year 2011. IB averages: 37.35 (2014), 38.2 (2015), 37 (2016)
The strongest K–12 leadership chapter. Five years managing a team of sixteen, building systems that scaled, and delivering measurable impact in a high-performing school.
  • 12% increase in senior school attainment within one year through targeted intervention
  • 300+ admissions interviews per year; built the assessment framework (NGRT, writing, listening, standardized packet)
  • Created the college’s Rolling Professional Development plan
  • Managed and mentored 16 staff (12 TAs, 4 teachers)
  • Founded Language Perfect competitive team: 3rd in the world, 1st place individual (2014)
  • Represented EAL on the Curriculum Committee; aligned EAL vertically and horizontally
  • Originated English for Parents, English for Teaching Assistants, Language Enrichment Group
  • Organized beta testing for blended IELTS platform (CollegePre / Cambridge University Press / Cambridge Assessment)
  • Extended Essay Supervisor, CAS Supervisor, Year 11 mentor, Form Tutor
  • Taught KS3 EAL, IGCSE EAL, KS3 English, IB English B, SEN support, Intensive English, PSHE
2016 — 2018

Director of Student Services, N–13

Harrow International School Shanghai · China
British international school, part of the Harrow family. Ages 18 months to 18 years.
Built the entire student services department from nothing. This was a ground-up construction project: framework, pathways, systems, training, and culture, all delivered within twelve months.
  • Built whole-school student services from inception within 12 months (N through Year 13)
  • Trained K–12 faculty in inclusive practice, CLIL methodology, and differentiation
  • Transformed admissions through data-informed assessment; 300+ assessments annually
  • Developed and implemented Individualized Education Plans
  • Coordinated Early Years, Junior, and Senior school transitions and placement
  • Key member of the school leadership team: strategic and operational planning
  • Partnered with families, teachers, external providers on student support
2018 — 2027

Senior Lecturer · DEI Faculty Chair · Center for Student Belonging Facilitator

NYU Shanghai · China
China’s first Sino-US research university. Third degree-granting campus of NYU Global Network. ~2,000 students from 70+ countries. LEED Gold New Bund Campus.
Progressed through Adjunct Faculty, Lecturer, and Senior Lecturer. Designed four interdisciplinary first-year courses, led institutional DEI and belonging work, mentored underrepresented students, and built the curriculum infrastructure for a program serving two thousand students from seventy countries.
  • Designed 4 innovative courses: Future Focused, (Un)Sustainability, Systems Thinking, History of Rock and Roll
  • DEI Faculty Chair: equity strategy, faculty engagement, curriculum review, community-building
  • Led inaugural Culture and Heritage Exposition as Community Connections Chair
  • Workshop facilitator, Center for Student Belonging
  • Co-facilitated LEAD mentorship for first-generation and underrepresented students (8 mentees)
  • Recruiter, advisor, and referee for the UN Millennium Fellowship (4,000+ fellows, 290+ campuses, 47 countries)
  • Served on Academic Program Review committees
  • Co-founded NYU Shanghai Sustainability Committee (LEED Gold campus)
  • Event Ambassador, No Translation Needed
  • Advisor, Collective Voice Student Debate; advisor/performer, faculty-student production of The Great Gatsby
