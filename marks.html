01

The record

1992–1996

German Teacher, Grades 7–12

Springfield High School · Vermont, USA

Four years teaching secondary German and English in a rural public school, including children of American soldiers from the Vietnam War and a behavioral support program. This was where I first learned that student agency begins with trust, structure, and seeing each learner as a whole human being.

1997–1999

Cambridge English & IELTS Instructor

The School for New Languages · Aarau, Switzerland

Taught Cambridge English and IELTS from kindergarten through adult professionals in German-speaking Switzerland, deepening the bilingual and intercultural practice that would shape the rest of my career.

2000–2003

Published Columnist · Curriculum Developer · Volunteer Literacy Teacher

The Bangkok Post · Mon Culture & Literature Survival Project · Thailand

Wrote the weekly Kat's Window on Thailand column for Asia's second-largest English-language newspaper, developing an educational voice rooted in public writing, community learning, and social literacy.

Volunteered with the Mon Culture and Literature Survival Project in Sangkhlaburi, building a 36-unit bilingual curriculum and Mon-script fonts for a stateless refugee community.

2003–2006

IELTS Instructor · K–12 English Teacher · Teacher Trainer

Taylor College, Sydney · ILBC (International Language and Business Centre), Mandalay

Completed the MA TESOL at UNSW and taught across all year groups in Myanmar. Built a 60-hour teacher training program with the British Council and contributed to a literacy and language-learning culture that reached beyond one classroom.

2007–2011

Head of EAL · Head of House · Head of PSHE (Personal, Social, Health and Economic Education)

Bromsgrove International School · Bangkok, Thailand

Held combined academic and pastoral responsibility across the secondary school, leading EAL curriculum, student debate, international days, boarding, and CIS accreditation. This was the start of my institutional building work: systems, culture, and belonging at scale.

2011–2016

Professional Development Coordinator · Head of EAL

Dulwich College Beijing · China

Built the EAL department from scratch. Before I arrived, there was no department: students were simply pulled out of classes for ad hoc, unstructured instruction. I built the model, staff, curriculum, and evaluation cycle that made language support sustainable and academically rigorous.

2016–2018

Director of Student Services, Nursery–Year 13

Harrow International School Shanghai · China

Built whole-school student services from inception within twelve months, trained faculty in inclusive practice, CLIL, and differentiation, rebuilt admissions around the student experience, and designed systems to support learning equity at a major international school.

2018–Present

Senior Lecturer · Community Connections (DEI) Faculty Chair

NYU Shanghai · China

Started as an Adjunct Lecturer alongside my Harrow role, joined full-time as a Lecturer in 2018, and was promoted to Senior Lecturer in 2025. Designed four interdisciplinary first-year courses, led belonging and DEI work, built community-engaged learning with public outcomes, and created systems that connect academic learning to civic and student agency.

The most important aspect of my work is whom I teach, not what I teach.
02

Who I am

Unconventional.

No running water, baby chickens in my bedroom, eight moves before finishing school. Nothing about the path was obvious, and nothing since has been either.

Resourceful.

What that upbringing taught me. Cook with whatever's on hand. Build a curriculum from nothing. Build a house from nothing. Build a department from nothing.

Direct.

I say the thing. The room catches up later.

Builder.

What directness and resourcefulness produce: systems that didn't exist before I arrived and still run after I leave.

Integrated.

The mature version of building. Not one system, but six that feed each other. The hats are different. The head is the same.

Fierce.

About my students, about fairness, about doing it right. Quietly, consistently, and with receipts.

Unflinching.

I look at hard things and don't look away.

03

NYU Shanghai: four leadership themes

On paper, four jobs. In my week, one practice. Monday's course design sends students into the community on Wednesday. The community work surfaces a belonging gap on Thursday. Friday becomes a systems-design session. The work is never isolated; it is integrated leadership.

01 · CURRICULUM

Builds learning systems that matter

Designs rigorous, future-facing academic experiences from a blank page, then builds the infrastructure that makes them work.

  • Designed four interdisciplinary first-year courses with all original materials.
  • Awarded the Experiential Learning Curriculum Design Fellowship.
  • Integrates AI literacy, sustainability, systems thinking, career readiness, and academic English.
  • Built the DIEP reflective framework (Describe, Interpret, Evaluate, Plan) into every course.
  • Built experiential learning through community service, SDG campaigns, climate simulation, and student-designed democratic voting systems.
  • Founded The Kitty Committee (293 members) as a student-led, self-funded experiential learning project.
  • 500+ first-year students from 70+ countries taught since 2018.
  • Co-authored the EAP Academic Program Review Self-Study and the department's strategic vision and five-year plan.
02 · BELONGING

Turns inclusion into institutional practice

Moves beyond statements about belonging and builds the teaching, mentoring, and faculty systems that make it visible.

  • Community Connections (DEI) Faculty Chair.
  • Facilitator for the Center for Student Belonging.
  • Co-facilitator of LEAD mentorship for first-generation and underrepresented students, with eight mentees.
  • Led the inaugural Culture and Heritage Exposition and advised Ally Week programming.
03 · COMMUNITY IMPACT

Connects classrooms to the real world

Designs experiential learning that produces public outcomes, not just assignments students forget after the semester ends.

  • Built a community-engaged model embedding assessed service into coursework.
  • Sustained partnerships with Stepping Stones Shanghai, Good-hope Bakery, Shanggang Elder Nursing Home, and student organizations.
  • Campus Point of Contact for the Millennium Fellowship; Associate Member of the MCN Civic Learning Council.
  • Taught and organized Dulwich College Beijing's World's Largest Lesson (2015), launching the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
  • Co-founded the NYU Shanghai Sustainability Committee.
  • Advisor to Green Shanghai (2018–2020) and Collective Voice Debate (2019–2021, 2024).
04 · LEADERSHIP

Leads change with evidence and purpose

Combines operational discipline with a clear moral purpose: better systems, stronger students, and decisions that can survive scrutiny.

  • Co-authored the institutional self-study and the department's strategic vision and five-year plan with the Assistant Dean.
  • Benchmarked EAP against DKU (Duke Kunshan University), XJTLU (Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University), and UNNC (University of Nottingham Ningbo China).
  • Completed Generative AI in Education at the University of Glasgow.
  • Completing the NPQH (National Professional Qualification for Headship) at UCL (University College London) Institute of Education.
  • Completed Designated Safeguarding Lead Training.
  • Member, Space Use Committee.
